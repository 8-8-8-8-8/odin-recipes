Parmesan-Roasted Tomatoes

These Parmesan tomatoes are a delicious vegetable side dish that go with pretty much anything.

Ingredients

  • 6 small tomatoes, halved
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 pinch salt
  • ground black pepper to taste
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F (200 degrees C).
  2. Place tomatoes in a bowl and toss gently with olive oil and season with salt and pepper. Arrange on a baking sheet and top each tomato half with Parmesan cheese.
  3. Bake in the preheated oven until Parmesan cheese is melted and slightly browned, 15 to 20 minutes.
